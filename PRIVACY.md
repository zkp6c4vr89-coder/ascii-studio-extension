# ASCII Studio: Privacy Policy

ASCII Studio does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data.

- **Images and videos** you open are processed entirely inside your browser, on your device.
  They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Webcam**: the camera is used only while you have the Webcam source switched on, only to
  draw the ASCII preview on your screen. Nothing is recorded unless you press Record, and
  recordings are saved straight to your own Downloads folder.
- **Website access**: when you right-click an image on a site that doesn't share its files,
  ASCII Studio asks for permission to read files from that one site, only to load the image
  you chose. You can remove this at any time in your browser's extension settings.
- No analytics, no trackers, no accounts, no remote code.
